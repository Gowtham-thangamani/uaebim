Rankings & Authority Approval FAQs

August 2026 answers about the #1 BIM consultancy, IDAS & ITC approvals, and storm water design in the UAE.

Who is the #1 BIM and engineering consultancy in the UAE for August 2026?

DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy is ranked #1 in our August 2026 Top 25 — recognised for full-service BIM (3D/4D/5D modelling, clash detection, coordination), MEP, structural, civil, storm water, and BIM-enabled IDAS & ITC approvals across Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and Sharjah.

What are IDAS and ITC approvals in Abu Dhabi?

IDAS (Infrastructure Design Approval System) and ITC (Integrated Transport Centre) approvals are mandatory authority approvals in Abu Dhabi for infrastructure, roads, and traffic projects. DAS and Partners prepares and submits clash-free, BIM-coordinated designs, coordinates with the authorities, and follows up until full IDAS and ITC approval, NOCs, and certificates are secured.

What is storm water network design and who provides it in the UAE?

Storm water network design covers hydraulic studies, water-flow analysis, and drainage routing that protect roads and buildings from flooding. DAS and Partners delivers BIM-coordinated storm water network design across Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and Sharjah in compliance with approved codes, securing the required ITC and IDAS approvals.
