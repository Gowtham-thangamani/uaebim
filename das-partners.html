BIM Services

DAS and Partners provides comprehensive BIM services across the project lifecycle.

▢

3D BIM Modeling

High-detail architectural, structural, and MEP BIM models in Revit, ArchiCAD, and Tekla. LOD 100 through LOD 500 deliverables for all building types and scales.

✓

Clash Detection

Automated and manual clash detection using Navisworks and BIM 360. Interdisciplinary coordination to resolve MEP, structural, and architectural conflicts before construction.

⚙

Digital Twins

Creating intelligent digital replicas of physical assets for real-time monitoring, predictive maintenance, and operational optimization throughout the building lifecycle.

☰

4D/5D Scheduling

Time-linked BIM models for construction sequencing (4D) and cost estimation integration (5D). Visual progress tracking and resource planning for complex projects.
