Our Story

Pioneering BIM Excellence Since 2009

UAE BIM was founded with a vision to transform the construction industry in the UAE through innovative Building Information Modeling solutions. Over 15 years, we have grown from a small team of BIM enthusiasts to a leading BIM consultancy serving major construction projects across the region.

Our team of 50+ certified BIM professionals combines deep industry expertise with cutting-edge technology to deliver accurate, efficient, and cost-effective BIM services. We are ISO 19650 certified and committed to excellence in every project we undertake.
