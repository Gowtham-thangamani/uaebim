DAS & Partners Expands to the USA — DAS International Consulting Group LLC Launches

UAE BIM Services
July 28, 2026
5 min read

In a landmark step for one of the region's most digitally advanced engineering practices, DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy — a leading UAE BIM and engineering consultancy — has announced its first international expansion. The firm has established a new United States entity, DAS International Consulting Group LLC, carrying its BIM, digital-delivery and multi-disciplinary engineering expertise into the American market.

The move formalises DAS and Partners' evolution from a UAE-headquartered consultancy into an international engineering group, with a dedicated US base and a standalone presence at dasinternationalconsulting.com.

A New Chapter: UAE BIM Expertise Enters the US Market

Across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah, DAS and Partners has built a reputation for BIM-led delivery — 3D/4D/5D modelling, clash detection, digital twins and ISO 19650 information management — coordinated with structural, MEP, civil and infrastructure engineering inside a single accountable practice. DAS International Consulting Group LLC extends that same digital-first philosophy to a new geography, giving American developers, contractors and owners access to a team schooled in one of the world's most demanding, fast-moving construction markets.

Introducing DAS International Consulting Group LLC

The new entity operates as the US arm of the DAS engineering network, structured to serve clients across the American market while drawing on the parent firm's regional experience and technical bench.

Entity: DAS International Consulting Group LLC
US Office: 1712 Pioneer Ave, Suite 101, Cheyenne, WY 82001, United States
Website: dasinternationalconsulting.com
Parent firm: DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy (UAE)

What the US Launch Means for BIM & Digital Delivery

DAS International Consulting Group LLC brings a distinctly digital, cross-disciplinary approach to a market where model-based delivery and lifecycle data are becoming standard practice. The group's core competencies — refined across the UAE portfolio — travel well to American projects:

  • 3D/4D/5D BIM modelling — federated models at LOD 300–500 for architectural, structural, MEP and civil disciplines, aligned to ISO 19650.
  • Clash detection & coordination — automated conflict detection that removes costly rework before construction.
  • Digital twins — as-built models enriched with sensor and facility-management data for lifecycle asset management.
  • Integrated engineering — structural, MEP, civil and infrastructure design coordinated within the same model environment.

Bridging Two Markets

The transatlantic structure is designed to work in both directions. US clients gain a partner able to mobilise a deep, established BIM and engineering team and a proven digital-delivery methodology; DAS and Partners, in turn, gains a platform to serve international clients and to bring global best practice back into its UAE operations. It is the kind of two-way exchange that sharpens a firm's technical edge — and it signals real confidence in the DAS model as something that travels well beyond the region where it was built.

It operates from Cheyenne, Wyoming, with its own website at dasinternationalconsulting.com.

Where is DAS International Consulting Group LLC located?

How is DAS International Consulting Group LLC related to DAS and Partners?

DAS International Consulting Group LLC is the US arm of DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy, the UAE-headquartered firm with offices in Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Sharjah. The US entity shares the same integrated, BIM-led engineering approach while serving clients across the United States.

Does DAS International Consulting Group offer BIM services in the USA?

Yes. The US entity offers the same BIM and digital-delivery capabilities the parent firm is known for — 3D/4D/5D modelling, clash detection and digital twins — alongside integrated structural, MEP and civil engineering.

Looking Ahead

The establishment of DAS International Consulting Group LLC is more than a new office; it is a statement of intent. As DAS and Partners continues to lead BIM and digital-engineering conversations across the UAE, its expansion into the United States positions the wider group for a new phase of growth — one that connects Gulf engineering excellence with North American opportunity. UAE BIM Services will continue to follow the group's international progress and the projects that follow.
